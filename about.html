ABOUT THE GUILD

> One server, born in Chefchaouen. The quest: enjoy, learn, teach, inspire.

◤ OUR STORY ◥

In December 2025, Riyad pressed “create server” with one simple plan: build a place where learning to code is actually fun. No gatekeeping, no boring lectures, no pretending to be a big corporation — just people enjoying code together.

The club runs 100% online, rooted in Chefchaouen, Morocco — but the crew already spans countries and time zones. Whether you're writing your first <hello, world!> or architecting whole systems, there's a seat at the table.

Our whole manifesto fits in four words: enjoy, learn, teach, inspire. Coding here feels like a co-op game, not a solo grind.
